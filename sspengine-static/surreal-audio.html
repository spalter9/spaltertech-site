WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Bass stays exactly where it was

Most phone "surround" effects widen the whole signal, bass included — which is exactly why they turn hollow or thin on a phone speaker or in mono. Surreal Audio splits below 150 Hz and only widens above it, the same move mastering engineers pay for in professional tools, not something built into stock phone effects.

Nothing gets louder or more compressed

This only ever changes the space, never the volume. It doesn't fight the mix the original engineer already balanced.

Proof, not a black box

The same phase-correlation meter from the Master Trust Engine console is right there on the demo — watch the bass stay locked near +1 while the top end opens up, instead of just trusting a toggle.
